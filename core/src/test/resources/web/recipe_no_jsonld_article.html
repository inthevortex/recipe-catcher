Rustic French Onion Soup

This classic French onion soup features deeply caramelized onions simmered in rich beef broth, topped with toasted baguette slices and melted Gruyère cheese.

Caramelizing the onions slowly over 45 minutes is the key to unlocking maximum sweetness and depth of flavor without burning.

Ingredients

5 large yellow onions, thinly sliced

3 tablespoons unsalted butter

1 tablespoon olive oil

1/2 cup dry white wine

6 cups rich beef stock

1 fresh baguette, sliced and toasted

1 1/2 cups grated Gruyère cheese

Instructions

In a heavy Dutch oven, melt the butter with olive oil over medium-low heat. Add the sliced onions and cook for 45 to 50 minutes, stirring occasionally until golden brown and caramelized.

Deglaze the pot with the white wine, scraping up all browned bits from the bottom.

Pour in the beef stock and simmer gently for 25 minutes. Season with salt and freshly cracked black pepper.

Ladle hot soup into oven-safe crocks, float toasted baguette slices on top, and generously cover with grated Gruyère cheese.

Broil for 3 to 5 minutes until the cheese is melted, bubbling, and golden brown. Serve immediately.
